# Relevance Rules

Score relevance against the campaign criteria you were given, not against your
own sense of what is interesting.

## Scoring bands

| Band   | Meaning                                                                       |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 90-100 | Directly and substantively about the campaign's include topics.               |
| 75-89  | Clearly related; a practitioner in this field would find it on topic.          |
| 60-74  | Adjacent or partially relevant; touches the topic but is mostly about something else. |
| 40-59  | Weak connection; shares vocabulary but not subject matter.                     |
| 0-39   | Unrelated, or matches an exclude topic.                                        |

## Hard rules

- Any match against the campaign's exclude topics or exclude keywords caps the
  score at 39 and makes the item ineligible, regardless of other merit.
- Recruitment posts, job advertisements, "we're hiring", and candidate sourcing
  are never eligible unless the campaign explicitly includes recruitment.
- Generic motivational content, engagement bait ("agree?", "comment YES"),
  reposts with no added commentary, pure self-promotion and advertisements are
  never eligible.
- Length is not quality. A short, specific technical observation scores higher
  than a long, vague one.
- A post that merely mentions a topic in passing is adjacent, not relevant.
- Judge the substance, not the author's seniority or follower count.

## Consistency

- The same input must produce the same score. Do not vary by mood or phrasing.
- Justify the score in `reason` by naming the concrete evidence you used. Say
  what the content is actually about; do not restate the criteria back.
