# Formatting Rules

- Keep list entries short: a title, a topic or a keyword, not a sentence.
- Normalise capitalisation to how the term is conventionally written
  ("Spring Boot", not "spring boot"; "CTO", not "Cto").
- Deduplicate list entries. Do not list both "AI" and "Artificial Intelligence"
  unless the user's instruction clearly distinguishes them.
- Preserve the user's own vocabulary where they were explicit. Do not silently
  broaden "senior Java developer" into "developer", and do not narrow it either.
- Do not add criteria the user did not ask for. An empty list is the correct
  answer when the instruction says nothing about that dimension.
- Keep every list under 40 entries.
